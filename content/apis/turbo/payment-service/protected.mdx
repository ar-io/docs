          **PROTECTED ROUTE** - Requires `PRIVATE_ROUTE_SECRET` authentication

          **PROTECTED ROUTE** - Requires `PRIVATE_ROUTE_SECRET` authentication
          header.


          Creates an approval allowing another address to spend credits from the
          paying address's balance.

          This enables delegated payment scenarios where one user pays for
          another user's uploads.


          **Authorization:** `x-auth-token` header must match
          `PRIVATE_ROUTE_SECRET` environment variable.
