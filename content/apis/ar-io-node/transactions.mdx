          Submits a signed L1 transaction (JSON), as an Arweave node's
          `POST /tx` does. Envoy sends it on to Arweave nodes: the peers it
          discovers by default, or `TRUSTED_NODE_HOST` and `FALLBACK_NODE_HOST`
          with `ENABLE_ARWEAVE_PEER_EDS=false`. With `ARWEAVE_POST_DRY_RUN=true`
          the gateway answers it itself: with
          `ARWEAVE_POST_DRY_RUN_SKIP_VALIDATION=true` it accepts anything,
          otherwise it checks the required fields and the signature, and it
          broadcasts nothing. Reaching the gateway directly (port 4000), it
          forwards to `TRUSTED_NODE_URL` itself. The gateway's own handler
          accepts bodies up to 10 MB.
