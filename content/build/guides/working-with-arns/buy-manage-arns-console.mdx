The [ar.io Console](https://console.ar.io) registers ArNS names and manages them alongside uploads, deploys and credits. This guide describes Console 4.11.0. To use the standalone ArNS app instead, see [Purchasing an ArNS name using arns.ar.io](/build/guides/working-with-arns/purchase-arns-ui).

## Before you start

An ArNS name is owned by a **Solana wallet**. The Console gets one for you in one of these ways:

- **Sign in with a Solana wallet**, such as Phantom, Solflare, or the Solana account in MetaMask. That wallet owns your names.
- **Sign in with email.** The Console creates a Solana wallet for your account.
- **Sign in with an Arweave or Ethereum wallet, then link a Solana wallet.** Your signed-in wallet stays your account and pays with its credits; the linked Solana wallet owns the names and approves changes to them. Link one from the Account page, or when the checkout asks.

**Credits are spent from the account you signed in with**, not from the wallet that owns the name. On an Arweave or Ethereum session, top up the signed-in account, not the linked Solana wallet.

## Register a name

<Steps>
 <Step>
  Go to [console.ar.io/arns](https://console.ar.io/arns) and search for the name you want. If it is available, choose a **lease** of 1 to 5 years or a **permanent** registration.
 </Step>

 <Step>
  Choose how to pay:

  - **Credits**: paid from your credit balance.
  - **Card**: paid by card through Stripe.
  - **Crypto**: a token your signed-in wallet can send, such as SOL or USDC on Solana. The token buys credits, and the credits pay for the name.
  - **ARIO**: pays the ArNS registry directly, with no Turbo infrastructure fee, and is marked "Best price" when it is the cheapest route for that name.

  With credits, a card, or a token other than ARIO, Turbo pays the Solana network costs. **Paying with ARIO is a transaction from your own wallet, so that wallet needs a little SOL** for Solana account rent and the network fee. The checkout shows the amount before you pay.
 </Step>

 <Step>
  Approve the purchase in your wallet. The name appears under [My domains](https://console.ar.io/my-domains).
 </Step>
</Steps>

### If a purchase does not finish

If you close the wallet prompt, approve too late, or leave the page mid-purchase, My domains shows what happened to that attempt and its credits: when they return to your balance, or that they are already back, with a **Try again** button. The notice appears in the browser where you started the purchase, for 30 minutes.

## Manage a name

Open a name from [My domains](https://console.ar.io/my-domains), or go to `console.ar.io/domains/NAME`. Replace NAME with the name. Each section of the page has its own actions:

| Section | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | **Renew or upgrade** a lease: extend it, make it permanent, or add undername slots. A permanent name has **Add undername slots** |
| Details | **Edit** the name's nickname, ticker, logo, description, and keywords |
| Ownership | **Transfer** the name to another wallet, **Reassign** it to a different name token, or **Release** a permanent name back to the registry |
| Controllers | **Manage** the wallets allowed to edit its records |
| Records | **Add record**, edit a record with its pencil, or remove it |

The header also has **Set as primary**, which makes the name your primary name, and **Visit**, which opens it.

Transfer, Reassign, and Release are available only to the owner, and each asks you to confirm before it goes ahead. A transfer cannot be undone.

### Edit records

The Records table lists the root record (`@`, the name itself) and every undername. Undernames use an underscore: `UNDERNAME_NAME.ar.io`. Select a record's name to open what it serves.

<Steps>
 <Step>
  To change where the name points, select the pencil on the `@` row. To add an undername, select **Add record**.
 </Step>

 <Step>
  Enter the target transaction ID and a TTL, then save. The change shows in the table as soon as it is saved, while the page confirms it against the chain.
 </Step>
</Steps>

### Who pays for a change

Record changes, transfers, and controller changes have a small cost, paid one of two ways:

- **If the wallet that owns the name holds enough SOL**, it signs the change and pays the Solana network fee, usually a fraction of a cent. **Pay with credits instead** switches to credits when both would work.
- **Otherwise, credits pay**, and Turbo pays the Solana fee. A wallet with no SOL, such as a new email account, always uses credits.

The cost is shown before you confirm. IPFS targets and record priority are always signed by the owning wallet and need a little SOL; the editor says so before you save.

**Controllers** can edit records, but cannot transfer, reassign, release, or change controllers. A controller always signs and pays the Solana fee from their own wallet.

Renewing, upgrading, and adding undername slots are paid like a purchase: with credits, a card, a token, or ARIO.

## Assign a name to an upload, deploy or capture

After you upload a file, deploy a site, or capture a page in the Console, you can point one of your names at it. When the name is assigned, the success message has **Visit**, which opens the name, and **Manage NAME**, which opens the name's page.

## Gateway operators

A gateway's operator wallet, and its operations wallet, get a 20% discount on ArNS purchases, renewals, upgrades, and undername slots **when paying with ARIO**. The checkout shows the discount as its own line. With credits, a card, or another token, Turbo pays the registry, and the discount does not apply.
